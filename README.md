# Pipe Slots

A tiny Mario-inspired scheduler. You list call times. Each time becomes a green warp pipe. Your client walks a little red-cap jumper over and hops in. That pick emails you.

This is an original plumber-style character and generic pipes — not Nintendo assets.

## Use it

1. Open the page (GitHub Pages or just `index.html`).
2. Enter **your email** and one time per line.
3. Click **Make share link** and send that URL.
4. The first time FormSubmit delivers to a new address, you get a confirmation email from them — click it once.

Controls: ← → move, ↑ or space jump. On a phone, use the on-screen pad. Stand on a pipe (centered) to warp in.
